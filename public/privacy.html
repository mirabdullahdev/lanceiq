Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

This policy explains what LanceWise collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is deliberately short, because this website collects very little.

What we collect

Your email address, and nothing else. It is collected only when you voluntarily submit it through the waitlist form on this site.

We do not collect your name, your location, your IP address, your payment details or any other personal information through this website. We do not use cookies to identify you, and we do not build a profile of you.

Why we collect it

By joining the waitlist, you consent to us using your email address to:

  • send you a single confirmation email containing your discount code;
  • notify you when LanceWise becomes available; and
  • send occasional updates about the product's release.

We will not use your email address for any other purpose without asking you first.

Where it is stored

Waitlist entries are held in a secured database, and email is delivered through an established email service provider. Both operate strictly on our instructions and may not use your email address for their own purposes. Your email address is not accessible to the public.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We do not sell, rent, trade or otherwise disclose your email address to third parties. The only exception is the service providers described above, who process it solely in order to store it and deliver mail on our behalf, and who are bound to do nothing else with it.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until the waitlist is no longer relevant to the product's launch, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You may at any time ask us to confirm what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it entirely. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also write to us directly at hello@lancewise.com. We will action deletion requests promptly and without asking you to justify them.

Analytics

If website analytics are enabled, they are cookieless and aggregate only. They record page views, not people, and cannot be used to identify you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects how your email address is used, we will tell the people on the waitlist by email rather than quietly editing this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything we hold, can be sent to hello@lancewise.com.

LanceWise is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Upwork Global Inc.
